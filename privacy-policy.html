Privacy Policy — Logger Helper

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Logger Helper is a Chrome extension that provides copy-paste templates, date and work-day calculations, a consumption calculator, a text comparison tool, personal notes, and lists of shortcut links — all running directly in the browser. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data when you use it.

Summary: Logger Helper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data with anyone — including the developer. Everything you enter stays on your own computer, inside your browser's local storage.

What data the extension handles

Logger Helper stores the following locally, using the browser's built-in localStorage (Web Storage) API:

  • Custom log entries, notes, and copy-paste templates you create
  • Link lists you configure (e.g. handbook links, formula links, whitelabel links)
  • The initials you enter, which are inserted into copied log lines

The Update Info page lets you load a shared JSON file to refresh these lists. The file is read from your own computer, or pasted in by you, and is processed entirely inside the browser tab. It is never uploaded anywhere.

What we don't do

  • We do not send any data to a remote server. The extension makes no network requests of any kind — this can be verified directly in the source code, which does not include a single fetch, XMLHttpRequest, or similar network call.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party, because we never receive it in the first place.
  • We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness, for lending purposes, or for personalized advertising.

Permissions this extension requests

None. Logger Helper's manifest declares no permissions at all — no host permissions, no optional permissions. It runs no content scripts, does not read the content of the web pages you visit, and has no access to your browsing history.

The two features that might look as though they need a permission do not. Copy buttons use the browser's standard asynchronous Clipboard API, which is available to any page you have clicked on and requires no declared permission. Your notes and lists are saved with the browser's own localStorage, which likewise needs no permission.

Data retention and deletion

All data described above lives only in your local browser profile. Uninstalling the extension, or clearing your browser's site/extension data for Logger Helper, permanently deletes it. We have no copy of it anywhere, so there is nothing further for us to delete on request.

Children's privacy

Logger Helper is an internal productivity tool intended for adult, professional use and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.
