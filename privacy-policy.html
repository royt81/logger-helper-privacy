Privacy Policy — Logger Helper

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Logger Helper is a Chrome extension that provides copy-paste templates, date-calculation utilities, and a set of self-contained productivity tools (a billing PDF reader, CSV balance reporter, ticket-link generator, and note lists) for use directly in the browser. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data when you use it.

Summary: Logger Helper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data with anyone — including the developer. Everything you enter stays on your own computer, inside your browser's local storage.

What data the extension handles

Logger Helper stores the following locally, using the browser's built-in chrome.storage and localStorage APIs:

  • Custom log entries, notes, and copy-paste templates you create
  • Link lists you configure (e.g. handbook links, formula links, whitelabel links)
  • Your display preferences (such as text size and initials used in log entries)
  • Ticket numbers you paste in, for generating a spreadsheet of ticket links

The billing PDF reader and CSV balance reporter tools read files you choose to open (PDF or CSV) and process them entirely inside your browser tab to generate a report. These files are never uploaded anywhere.

What we don't do

  • We do not send any data to a remote server. The extension makes no network requests of any kind — this can be verified directly in the source code, which does not include a single fetch, XMLHttpRequest, or similar network call.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party, because we never receive it in the first place.
  • We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness, for lending purposes, or for personalized advertising.

Permissions this extension requests

  • storage — used to save the notes, lists, and preferences described above locally in your browser, so they persist between sessions.
  • clipboardWrite — used only when you click a copy button, to place the corresponding template text or log line onto your clipboard.

No other permissions are requested. Logger Helper does not read the content of the web pages you visit and does not have access to your browsing history.

Data retention and deletion

All data described above lives only in your local browser profile. Uninstalling the extension, or clearing your browser's site/extension data for Logger Helper, permanently deletes it. We have no copy of it anywhere, so there is nothing further for us to delete on request.

Children's privacy

Logger Helper is an internal productivity tool intended for adult, professional use and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date above.
